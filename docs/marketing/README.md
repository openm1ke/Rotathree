# RotaThree promotional media

- [30-second gameplay trailer](rotathree-30s-square.mp4) — 1080 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4 with stereo AAC audio; approximately 3.6 MB. English captions and a Playgama call to action.
- [Trailer cover](rotathree-cover.jpg) — 1080 × 1080 JPEG, taken from the video.
- [Playable game on Playgama](https://playgama.ai/play/vfwzsuyxeyxjut).

The trailer shows matching three blocks of one colour, rotating between two wells, and managing four wells. The final seconds show the RotaThree wordmark and playable link.

Footage comes from the production TypeScript engine and renderer. A capture-only player supplies normal movement, rotation, well selection and drop inputs; boards and scores are produced by the game. The three scenes are separate runs edited together, with screen shake disabled for clarity.
